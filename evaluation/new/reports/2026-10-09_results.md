## 2. Analysis

**The detector, not the tracker, limits the real system on the flock.** With `yolo` input on
1DAC, every configuration matches only 13.8 to 15.1% of the GT boxes (CLEAR recall `CLR_Re`,
i.e. 1,400 to 1,530 of 10,142 boxes with a tracker box within DotD 0.5; HOTA's detection recall
`DetRe`, which averages over many match thresholds, gives 0.135 to 0.147). HOTA stays between
0.26 and 0.27 for every association method except pure `appearance` (0.187). In the dense part of
the clip the detector's boxes are about 2.4 times larger than the birds and often cover several of them, so
there is little left for the tracker to get right. The cloud tracker, which uses the same detector
(about 80% of its boxes are YOLO boxes), lands in the same place on 1DAC (HOTA 0.238, recall 13.9%).

**On 6C42, a higher confirmation threshold is the one change that clearly helps.** The
`k4t20_*` configurations set `tentative_threshold = 20` (vs the default 3: a new track needs 20
matched detections before it is confirmed and reported; `max_age` stays at 30) together with
`kalman_gating = full`. They reach HOTA 0.62 against 0.52 for baseline: they report 9 ids instead
of 35 for the single bird and halve the false boxes (about 205 vs 411), so the share of reported
boxes that are on the bird (CLEAR precision `CLR_Pr`) rises from 0.34 to 0.51. The gain comes from
the threshold, not the gating: `kalman_4d` (gating `full`, threshold 3) scores 0.515, the same as
baseline. This matches the
earlier note in `experiments/results/notes.md` that short false detections near the turbine are
being promoted into tracks. With that setting, the cloud tracker (0.644) and the best kit
configurations (0.621 to 0.624) are close on this clip. One exception: `k4t20_original` has the
same detection scores but a much worse identity on 6C42 (AssA 0.449 vs about 0.82, IDF1 0.409 vs
0.651), so the stricter Kalman gating with the original association cost splits this bird more.

**A high confirmation threshold also hides birds.** On 1DAC the `k4t20_*` runs (`tentative_threshold
= 20`) report 11 to 15
ids for 25 birds, with slightly more misses than baseline (CLR_FN about 8730 vs 8615). Their
better pooled HOTA on `yolo` comes from fewer false boxes, not from following more birds.

**With perfect detections, motion-only association is best and appearance hurts.** On `gt`
input, `motion` beats baseline (HOTA 0.981 vs 0.963, IDSW 14 vs 21). Every configuration that adds
the visual appearance term does worse, and more so the more weight it gets: with appearance
weight 0.25 (`fused_w025` 0.971, `k4t20_fused_w025` 0.970) the loss is small; with weight 0.5
(`fused` 0.845, `k4t20_fused` 0.818, and the single-cue variants 0.61 to 0.82) it is large; pure
`appearance` (weight 1) falls to 0.382. Pure appearance
keeps exactly 25 tracks but swaps birds about 7,000 times; each track sits on its most frequent
bird only 19% of the time (checked directly from the boxes). At 8 to 11 px, the birds of a flock
look alike, so the appearance cues add noise rather than identity. With `yolo` input the damage is
partly hidden by the detector, but `appearance` is still the worst configuration (0.205).

**Even the best tracker swaps nearly overlapping birds.** On 1DAC with `gt` input, baseline makes
83 identity jumps (counted exactly from the boxes); the standard `IDSW` reports 21, because it
keeps a pairing as long as it is still close enough. `diag_id_changes` (91 with `dotd`, 83 with
`iou`) tracks the exact count much better. The birds involved are a median 1.4 px apart.

**Variants behave as designed.** `matched_final`, `matched_online` and `matched_final_all` are
within 0.004 HOTA of each other. Adding the boxes written while a track has no detection costs a
lot: on `gt` input HOTA falls from 0.963 to 0.890 (all 844 such boxes are false by construction,
since GT has no box there), on `yolo` from 0.278 to 0.239. The `frozen` variant reproduces the old
result files exactly wherever both exist.

**The choice of similarity changes levels, not rankings.** With `gt` input, `dotd` and `iou` agree
within 0.003. With `yolo` input, `iou` scores 0.026 to 0.041 lower because the detector's boxes are
too large to overlap the tiny GT boxes well; the order of the experiments is almost the same (rank
correlation 0.98), only near-ties swap (e.g. `motion` and `baseline`). The
three `dotd` scales (`per_track`, `fixed`, `per_box`) differ by at most 0.006.

**Pooled and per-clip averages can disagree.** Pooled numbers follow 1DAC; `per_video_mean`
weights the single 6C42 bird like the whole flock. For example `k4t20_original` is 0.279 pooled
but 0.365 as a per-clip mean, and on `yolo` the best per-clip mean (0.447, `k4t20_fused_patch`) is
far above any pooled value. Report which one is meant.

## 3. Caveats

- Two clips only, one of them with a single bird. Differences of 0.01 HOTA on `yolo` are within
  what one more clip could reverse.
- `Frag` on 1DAC with `gt` input (about 110) is almost entirely caused by gaps in the hand
  labelling (110 of 110), not by the tracker.
- About 46% of the 6C42 GT boxes are exact copies of machine boxes: comparing coordinates frame
  by frame, 111 of 241 are identical to a cloud tracker box and 100 to a YOLO detection, 98 of
  them to both (the cloud tracker reuses YOLO boxes); 128 match neither. This suggests the
  labelling started from detector boxes where they were good, but the annotation workflow is not
  documented here. It slightly favours outputs that reuse those same boxes (`yolo` runs and the
  cloud tracker) on that clip, mostly in localisation (`LocA`, `MOTP`).
- `MOTA` is negative for most `yolo` runs because false boxes outnumber the GT boxes; it is not
  useful for ranking them.

## 4. What this suggests

- For the flock, improving the detector (separate boxes for close birds) would matter far more
  than any association setting tested here.
- A higher confirmation threshold (`tentative_threshold`) is worth keeping for false tracks near the turbine, but its cost
  in missed birds on dense scenes needs a check on more clips.
- Motion-only association is the strongest association method on clean detections; the
  appearance cues, as implemented, should not be used for these bird sizes.
